VAMP (150 bps Limit)

Consolidates TC40 fraud notices and TC15 chargebacks divided by TC05 settled transactions. Identifies Merchant Excessive breaches (≥ 1.50% & ≥ 1,500 events) and enumeration attacks (≥ 20% decline ratio).

ECP (M-1 Lagged Ratio)

Calculates Chargeback-to-Transaction Ratio using previous month's sales denominator. Tracks ECM (100–299 bps) and HECM (≥ 300 bps) with fine schedule tracking up to $200,000/mo and 3-month clean exit criteria.
