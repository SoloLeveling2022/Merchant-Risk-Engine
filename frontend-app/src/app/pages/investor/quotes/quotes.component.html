StockShield Policy Terms & Deductible Disclosure

Statutory conditions governing equity portfolio insurance

timelapse 1. Mandatory 40% Ceiled Lock-in Period & Option A Cliff Vesting

Every policy carries a dynamic tenure ({{ getTenureYears() }} Years) with a mandatory 40% ceiled lock-in period ({{ getWaitingPeriodYears() }} Years). Under Option A (Cliff Vesting), 100% of the insured coverage cap unlocks at the start of Year {{ getWaitingPeriodYears() + 1 }}, provided all annual premiums are paid on schedule.

trending_down 2. 20% Deductible / Market Crash Trigger

StockShield protects against severe systemic market drawdowns. Under platform actuarial rules, a claim is only admissible if total cumulative loss exceeds the 20% deductible threshold from the insured baseline valuation. Losses under 20% are absorbed as normal market volatility.

balance 3. Dynamic Re-Underwriting & Endorsement Tranches

You are permitted to rebalance or update stock quantities in your insured portfolio. When quantities change, the platform automatically re-evaluates risk and creates an Endorsement Tranche so your policy coverage seamlessly mirrors your actual holdings.
